About Me

My practice combines creative coding, physical computing, immersive media, and practice-based research. I am particularly interested in gesture-based interaction, multisensory experience, and the cultural and social assumptions embedded in technological systems.

Through interactive installations, virtual environments, and generative visual systems, I investigate how technology can respond to bodily movement, emotional experience, and forms of human and non-human communication.
