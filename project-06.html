The Safest Keyboard

The Safest Keyboard is a fictional safety product that uses exaggerated protective features to critique online censorship, sensitive-word restrictions and self-censorship. Presented through a physical keyboard prop and a short promotional film, the project asks what happens when promises of online safety begin to restrict access to information and personal expression.

  • Duration November–December 2022
  • Format Speculative product · 30-second film
  • Methods Critical design · Prop making · Video production
