Character & Icon Design · Collaborative Project · 2020

Military-style IP Design

Collaborators: 宋雪林 and 王安然

This is a set of IP character designs with a cute military style. Two characters with different states and silhouettes were developed.

Drawing on the characters and elements of military life, we created two sets of mobile phone icons with distinct visual styles.

The project also presents the colour palettes used for both characters and demonstrates their application across different scenarios, helping audiences understand their visual identities more clearly.
