Fear Trigger Box

Fear Trigger Box is a modular interactive installation exploring how digital devices trigger everyday fears related to surveillance, interruption, control and loss of agency. Six sensor-based modules translate abstract forms of social fear into physical actions, sound, movement and projected imagery.

  • Duration May–September 2022
  • Format Six interactive modules
  • Tools Arduino · TouchDesigner · Sensors · Servos
