Healing Fragments

A VR exploration game examining fracture recovery, embodied interaction, and emotional experience.

Project Overview

Healing Fragments is a VR rehabilitation experience that integrates physiotherapy movements into symbolic interactions, transforming therapeutic exercises into emotionally meaningful actions. Rooted in traditional Chinese aesthetics, the project creates a calm and supportive immersive environment that offers users emotional comfort through its visual and atmospheric design. Each gesture triggers symbolic feedback—such as an embrace, petals, lantern light, or flowing water—reinforcing the connection between body and emotion, and allowing the rehabilitation process to become both physically engaging and emotionally resonant.

Research Question

How can VR support both physical rehabilitation and emotional healing during fracture recovery?

Case Studies

Related Background and Theory

  • Fracture rehabilitation often involves psychological distress, including anxiety, fatigue, and emotional disconnection. (Brinkman et al., 2024)
  • Repetitive exercises reduce motivation when there is no emotional feedback or sense of progress. (Jack et al., 2010)
  • VR can reduce anxiety and increase engagement through immersion and presence. (Rizzo & Koenig, 2017)
  • Immersive visuals enhance emotional involvement and make training feel less repetitive. (Tunc et al., 2024)
  • VR relaxation is effective in clinical rehabilitation contexts, helping users feel calmer and more supported. (Edwards et al., 2025)

Development Process

Iteration 2: Improving Feedback & Responsiveness

  • Added clearer gesture feedback: pick flower → throw forward; raise arm → light lantern; rotate wrist → pour water.
  • Visual cues strengthened through flower particles, lantern flame, and water flow.
  • Trigger-based interactions introduced to increase clarity.

Final Iteration: Symbolism, Intelligibility & Emotional Tone

  • Added subtle sound cues, including flame and ocean waves, for emotional clarity.
  • Adjusted gesture thresholds for easier, more inclusive triggering.
  • Tuned visual and audio feedback to support a calm therapeutic rhythm.
  • Result: interactions became more reliable, gentle, and emotionally resonant.
